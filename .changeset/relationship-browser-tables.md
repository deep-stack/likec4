The relationships browser draws a table as its preview: its keys and the fields its relationships join, and as many more fields as fit. A relationship between tables is named by the fields it joins, as `order_id → id`. When many elements relate to the subject, their tall column is split into columns side by side, so the browser opens large enough to read.
