An ER table column can carry a badge, such as `badge "contact"` or `badge "health, special" warning`. Its row is tinted with a coloured edge and the badge sits in its own column; tables with no badge look as before. Embedding apps can set the colours with `--likec4-table-badge-color` and `--likec4-table-badge-warning-color`.
